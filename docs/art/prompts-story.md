# Cutscene Image Prompts for Story Chapters (Hồi 1 – Hồi 5)

Style guidelines for image generation:
- **Art Style:** 2D Chinese ink-brush (shuimo) painting on aged rice paper, sharp black ink brush outlines, flat translucent ink washes, rich vermilion red and ochre-gold accents.
- **Composition & Resolution:** Wide landscape format 1536x1024. Keep the lower 30% calmer, misty, and uncluttered to accommodate the narration text scroll overlay.
- **Strict Exclusions:** No text, no letters, no words, no signatures, no watermark, no modern artifacts.
- **Hero Appearance:** A young male wanderer in a grey-blue travelling robe, bamboo conical hat, cloth bundle tied at his waist, holding a plain steel sword.
